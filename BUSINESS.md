# ShieldDesk — Business Plan

> **The IT department small businesses can't afford.**
> ShieldDesk keeps small businesses online, backed up and POPIA-ready for a fixed monthly fee, with a live Resilience Score that shows exactly what is at risk and what to fix first.

---

## 1. The problem

South African small businesses (clinics, law and accounting offices, schools, construction and security firms, retail branches) with **5 to 50 staff** depend on IT every day, but almost none of them have an IT person.

What that looks like in practice:

- **Backups that nobody has ever tested.** The NAS "has been backing up" for two years, until the day it has to restore.
- **CCTV and biometric systems that fail silently.** A Hikvision DVR's disk dies and nobody notices for weeks. The time-and-attendance server crashes on payday.
- **Weak Microsoft 365 and network hygiene.** No MFA, shared admin accounts, flat networks, old firmware, ex-staff accounts still active.
- **POPIA exposure.** CCTV footage and biometric data are personal information (biometrics are *special* personal information). Most owners have no policy, signage or access control, and POPIA penalties can reach **R10 million**.
- **Load-shedding and connectivity failures** that stop work, with no one tracking what each outage costs.
- **Reactive, unpredictable IT spend.** They call a technician only when something is already broken, pay call-out rates, and get no record of what was done.

**Who has it:** nearly every small business that has outgrown "my nephew is good with computers" but cannot justify a managed service provider (MSP) retainer.
**How often:** continuously. Every outage, failed backup and unpatched device is a live risk.
**What it costs them today:** downtime, lost data, emergency call-out fees, staff who cannot work, and unquantified compliance risk.

## 3. The solution (one sentence)

ShieldDesk gives every small business a **Resilience Score**, a **service desk** and a **monthly health report** for a fixed monthly fee, backed by real technicians who fix what drags the score down.

**How it works**

1. The business signs up and registers its sites and assets (router, NAS, DVR, biometric device, M365 tenant).
2. A guided **Resilience Audit** covers backups, Microsoft 365 security, network, CCTV and biometrics, POPIA readiness and power/connectivity resilience. The result is a 0 to 100 score with prioritised fixes.
3. Issues become **tickets** assigned to a technician, with status, comments and history.
4. Every month the owner gets a **health report**: score trend, tickets resolved, response times and estimated downtime avoided.

**Growth hook:** a public **Free Resilience Check** that scores any business in about 2 minutes and captures the lead.

## 4. The impact

**What changes if we succeed**

- Small businesses stop discovering failures only after they happen.
- Fewer outages and data-loss events; backups are verified, not assumed.
- Owners understand and reduce their POPIA exposure.
- Local technicians get steady, structured, recurring work instead of ad hoc call-outs.

**How we measure it**

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Resilience Score uplift | Average score improvement per customer within 90 days |
| Verified backup restores | % of customers with a successful, recorded restore test |
| Mean time to first response | Ticket created to first technician response |
| Downtime avoided (est.) | Hours of outage prevented or shortened, from logged incidents |
| POPIA readiness | % of POPIA checklist items complete per customer |
| Customer retention | % of customers still active after 6 months |

**Target:** 10 customers in month 1 (pilot pricing), 30 by month 3, 100 by month 12.

## 7. The competition

| Alternative | Why customers use it | Why ShieldDesk is better for this segment |
|---|---|---|
| **Large MSPs** | Full-service IT | Minimum contracts and prices built for bigger companies; small clients are an afterthought |
| **Freelance technicians** | Cheap, personal | No tracking, no monitoring mindset, no continuity if the person is unavailable, no compliance view |
| **"Nephew who is good with computers"** | Free or cheap | Zero process, zero documentation, key-person risk |
| **RMM tools (NinjaOne, Datto and similar)** | Powerful monitoring | Built for MSPs, not for the business owner; priced in USD; no plain-language score or POPIA view |
| **Do nothing** | No cost today | Costs far more when something fails |

**Why ShieldDesk wins:** a plain-language score owners understand, a fixed rand price, local technicians, and a focus on the systems small businesses actually run (NAS, CCTV, biometrics, M365, load-shedding resilience) with POPIA built in.
